---
title: Silence and negative space
topic: silence
genre: general
source: SoundScript original notes (general film-scoring and music-theory knowledge, not a quotation of any book)
---
Removing music can be as powerful as adding it. Dropping the score just before a reveal, a death or a moment of shock makes the audience lean in and gives later cues more impact.

Silence works best as a contrast. A scene with music that stops abruptly feels different from one that never had any. Ambience, breathing, room tone and diegetic sound often carry the emotion during these gaps.

Plan where music is absent as deliberately as where it enters. Over-scoring every scene flattens emotional dynamics and can tell the audience what to feel instead of letting them feel it.
