---
title: Cues, transitions and timing
topic: transitions
genre: general
source: SoundScript original notes (general film-scoring and music-theory knowledge, not a quotation of any book)
---
A cue is a piece of music tied to a specific point in a film. A spotting session decides where each cue starts and ends. Cues often start on an emotional shift rather than at the start of a scene.

Transitions between scenes can use a sustained note or pad that bridges the cut, a crossfade between themes, or a stinger that punctuates a cut. A sound bridge carries audio from one scene into the next.

Hitting every on-screen action, known as mickey-mousing, is rarely suited to live-action drama. Music that moves subtly with the emotional arc generally works better than music that matches every movement.
