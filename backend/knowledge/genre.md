Horror often uses dissonant clusters, extended string techniques, sudden stingers and long silences. Romance favours warm strings, piano and memorable melodies. Science fiction uses synthesisers, unusual timbres and wide, spacious textures.

Action scores rely on fast tempos, driving percussion and heroic brass. Dramas lean on restrained underscoring, solo instruments and long emotional arcs. Animation often uses lively, colourful orchestration with strong themes.

Genre conventions are expectations. Following them gives clarity, while deliberately breaking them, such as a gentle lullaby over a horror scene, creates unease.
