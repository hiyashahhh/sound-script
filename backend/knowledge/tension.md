title: Tension and suspense

Sustained tension is commonly built from dissonant intervals such as the tritone and minor second, pedal tones held under changing harmony, and ostinatos that repeat without resolving. Tremolo strings and low sustained brass add unease.

Gradual rising pitch, accelerating rhythm and growing volume or density raise intensity over time. Withholding the expected resolution keeps the audience waiting.

Sparse textures can be tenser than loud ones. A single high held note, a ticking pulse or an unresolved chord can sustain suspense while leaving room for sound design and dialogue.
