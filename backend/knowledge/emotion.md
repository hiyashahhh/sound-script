title: Harmony, mode and emotion

Major keys tend to read as bright or resolved and minor keys as sad or serious, but modes add finer shades. Dorian is melancholic yet hopeful, Lydian is dreamy or wondrous, and Phrygian sounds dark and tense.

Unresolved or suspended harmony suggests longing or uncertainty. Cadences that resolve strongly create closure. Rising melodic lines often feel hopeful and falling lines resigned.

Mixed or ambiguous emotion is often best scored with restraint. A simple major melody over a sad scene, or a quiet minor figure over a happy one, can create irony and depth.
