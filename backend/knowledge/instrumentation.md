Strings are the flexible core of orchestral scoring: warm legato strings suit romance and grief, while short staccato strings suit urgency. A solo cello or violin gives an intimate, personal tone.

Brass suggests heroism, authority or menace, depending on register and dynamics. Low brass is ominous. Woodwinds such as flute, oboe and clarinet suit pastoral, delicate or wistful scenes. Piano is intimate and exposed.

Synthesisers and processed sounds create modern, alien or cold atmospheres. Solo voice, choir, percussion and unusual instruments such as the theremin each carry strong associations. Choose colour first, then arrange the melody for it.
