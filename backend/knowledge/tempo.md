---
title: Tempo, rhythm and texture
topic: tempo

Slow tempos of roughly 50 to 80 beats per minute suit calm, grief or reflection. Moderate tempos suit walking or conversation. Fast tempos above roughly 120 beats per minute suit chases, fights and urgency.

Rhythm shapes energy. Driving ostinatos, syncopation and percussion add momentum, while free rhythm or long sustained notes create stillness and suspension.

Texture describes how many layers sound at once. Thin texture feels isolated or intimate. Dense layered texture feels epic or overwhelming. Building from thin to dense across a cue is a reliable way to create a sense of rise.
