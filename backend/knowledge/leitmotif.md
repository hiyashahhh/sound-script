A leitmotif is a short, recognisable musical idea tied to a character, relationship, place or concept. The technique is associated with Wagner's operas and is central to film scores such as John Williams's Star Wars music.

Themes become meaningful through repetition and change. Reharmonising a theme in minor, slowing it, thinning the orchestration or fragmenting it can show a character's loss, corruption or growth without any dialogue.

Introduce a motif plainly the first time it is linked to its subject. Later variations only read as development if the audience can recognise the original. Use a motif sparingly so it keeps its weight.
